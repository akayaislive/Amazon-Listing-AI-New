Amazon listing guidelines at a glance

AKAYA AI follows these principles in every listing it writes.

Accurate claims onlyNo invented certifications, warranty, sizes or test results. If a detail is missing, it is left out.
No promotional wordsWords like best, free, sale or guaranteed are avoided, because Amazon expects factual content.
Keywords where they countThe main keyword goes near the start of the title. Other keywords are spread over highlights, bullets and description.
Mobile-first readingShort, clear lines that are easy to read on a phone, where most shoppers browse.
Clean formattingNo emojis, HTML or repeated words, and no competitor names.
Correct package contentOnly the items you list are mentioned, so buyers know exactly what they get.
